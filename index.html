Our Story

A family taco stand, not a chain.

Tacos Zempoal is a small, family-run taco stand on Mission Blvd in Pomona — handmade tortillas, meat grilled to order, and a menu kept short on purpose. Regulars come back a few times a week for the carne asada and al pastor, and the salsa gets called out by name.

Even the name has a story: Zempoal nods to Cempoala, an ancient city in Veracruz, Mexico. Nice bit of trivia — but people mostly come for the $2 tacos.
